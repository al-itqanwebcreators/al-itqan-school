School Activities

At Al-Itqan Science School, we believe that education goes beyond the classroom. Our students participate in a variety of academic, Islamic, cultural, sporting, and social activities that help develop their knowledge, confidence, talents, discipline, and leadership skills.

Academic Activities

-Quiz and debate competitions
-Mathematics and science competitions
-Reading and spelling activities
-Educational exhibitions
-Computer and ICT activities
-Academic presentations

Islamic Activities

-Qur'an memorization and recitation
-Hadith competitions
-Islamic knowledge competitions
-Islamic lectures and reminders
-Arabic language activities
-Seerah and Islamic history activities

Sports Activities

-Football
-Athletics
-Volleyball
-Basketball
-Indoor games
-Physical fitness activities

Cultural & Creative Activities

-Cultural presentations
-Poetry and public speaking
-Drama and educational performances
-Art and craft
-Essay and handwriting competitions
-Hausa, Arabic, and English language activities

Leadership & Social Activities

-Students' leadership programs
-Prefect activities
-Teamwork and group projects
-Community service
-Cleanliness and environmental activities
-School awareness programs

Competitions & Special Events

Our students take part in various school competitions, inter-house activities, educational events, Islamic programs, award ceremonies, and special celebrations throughout the academic year.

Our goal is to provide every student with opportunities to discover their talents, develop important life skills, and grow into responsible and confident individuals.
