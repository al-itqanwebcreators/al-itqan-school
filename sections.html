The School Sections

At Al-Itqan Science School, we provide a comprehensive and balanced educational system designed to nurture students academically, morally, socially, and spiritually.

Our school offers different educational sections to meet the needs of learners at various stages of their education. These include Islamic, Nursery, Primary, Junior Secondary, Senior Secondary, and a Combined Section that integrates Western and Islamic education.

Each section provides a supportive and stimulating learning environment where students are encouraged to acquire knowledge, develop good character, build practical skills, and prepare for a successful future.

Islamic Section

The Islamiyya Section focuses on Islamic knowledge and character development. Students are guided in Qur'anic studies, Hadith, Fiqh, Arabic language, Islamic values, morals, and good conduct.

Nursery Section

The Nursery Section provides a safe, caring, and engaging environment for young learners. Children are introduced to basic academic skills, communication, creativity, social interaction, good manners, and early Islamic values.

Primary Section

The Primary Section provides a strong foundation in education. Students develop essential skills in reading, writing, mathematics, science, communication, and other areas while being guided toward discipline, confidence, and good character.

Junior Secondary Section

The Junior Secondary Section builds upon the foundation established at the primary level. Students develop broader academic knowledge, critical thinking, practical skills, discipline, and greater responsibility as they prepare for senior secondary education.

Senior Secondary Section

The Senior Secondary Section provides advanced academic education and prepares students for higher education, professional opportunities, leadership, and responsible citizenship.

Combined Section

The Combined Section offers an integrated educational system that combines Western and Islamic education. It is designed to provide students with academic knowledge alongside sound Islamic education, values, morals, and character development.

Our Commitment

At Al-Itqan Science School, we believe that true education goes beyond academic achievement. We aim to develop students who are knowledgeable, disciplined, confident, responsible, and morally upright.

Through our different school sections, we strive to provide quality education that prepares every student to succeed in their studies and make a positive contribution to society.
