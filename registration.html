Registration Procedure

How to Register at Al-Itqan Science School

We have made the admission and registration process simple and straightforward. Parents
or guardians who wish to enroll their children at Al-Itqan Science School should follow the steps below:

  1. Obtain the Registration Form
    Visit the school or access the registration form through our official website. Complete the form with accurate information about the student and parent/guardian.
  2. Submit Required Documents
    Applicants should provide the necessary documents, which may include:
    • Student's birth certificate or age declaration
    • Previous school report/result
    • Passport photographs
    • Transfer certificate, where applicable
    • Parent/guardian identification
    • Other documents requested by the school
  3. Submit the Completed Form
    After completing the registration form, submit it together with the required documents to the school administration for verification.
  4. Entrance Assessment
    Depending on the class being applied for, the student may be required to take an entrance assessment or participate in an interview.
  5. Admission Decision
    The school will review the student's application and assessment results. Successful applicants will be notified of their admission.
  6. Payment of School Fees
    After receiving an admission offer, parents or guardians should complete the required school-fee payment within the specified period.
  7. Final Registration
    Once the required documents and payments have been completed, the student's registration will be confirmed.
  8. Resumption
    The student will receive information about the resumption date, class, school rules, timetable, uniform, and other important requirements.
Important Notice
Parents and guardians are encouraged to provide accurate and complete information during registration. Admission is subject to the availability of places and the school's admission requirements.

For further information
please contact the Al-Itqan Science School administration.
